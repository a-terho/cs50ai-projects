# Analysis

## Layer 2, Head 7

Head seems to have learned to pay attention to the main object of the sentence.
In these examples, the masked word is paid attention with almost every word.

Example Sentences:

- All my few remaining [MASK] were sold yesterday.
- Even after all this, I still have [MASK] with me.

## Layer 5, Head 6

Head seems to have learned to pay attention to words that they are referring
(verbs refer to objects: have -> idea, preposition refer to nouns: of ->
things, in -> inventory etc).

- Example Sentences:
- I have no idea what [MASK] is.
- They had [MASK] of things in their inventory.
